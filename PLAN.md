# 16h30 — Pâtisserie Boulangerie Seize Heures Trente — Research & Plan

> **16h30** (legal name *SEIZE HEURES TRENTE*, Google listing "PÂTISSERIE BOULANGERIE SEIZE HEURES TRENTE") is an artisan pâtisserie and boulangerie in Rennes, founded in 2018 by pastry chef **Marion Juhel**. The name is the time of the French *goûter* (afternoon snack). It has two sites. The original shop and small salon de thé is at **3 rue Hoche**. In July 2023 a second shop opened at **2 passage Jeanne-Chauveau**, in the new Hôtel-Dieu block. It sells bread, and the bakery can be seen working from the shop. Production moved there too. The house is known for being "responsable": mostly organic, local and Breton ingredients, everything made in-house, very little single-use packaging, and a 1 € deposit on jars. It won the **Trophée Pâtissier Gault&Millau Grand Ouest 2020** and an **Éco-Défis "coup de cœur" 2020** award. It is known for cakes de voyage, brioche feuilletée, kouign-amann, cookies, and seasonal creations with place names (Menton, Medellin, Bréhat, Cambrai…).

## 2. Story & history

- **2018** — Marion Juhel opens 16h30 alone at 3 rue Hoche. Ouest-France calls her "la pâtissière autodidacte qui a lancé, seule, 16 h 30 en 2018". Source: Ouest-France 2023 snippet.
- **Training:** the podcast summary says she apprenticed at **Philippe Bouvier**'s pâtisserie (Maison Bouvier, Rennes). One search summary called her a "former engineer turned pastry chef", but I could not trace the original source. **Low confidence — do not publish.**
- **Her stated aim:** a pâtisserie that is « régressive, moderne, responsable et abordable ». She describes it as « la pâtisserie la plus savoureuse et la plus responsable ». Source: [podcast "Ici vous êtes", 2021-10-02](https://podcast.ausha.co/ici-vous-etes/patisserie-16h30-chez-marion).
- **2020** — She wins the Gault&Millau Grand Ouest pastry trophy and the Éco-Défis coup de cœur. The Éco-Défis film shows the jar deposit and water-saving tap aerators.
- **2023 (Mar/Apr)** — Régal magazine n°112 (Rennes special issue) calls 16h30 a « maison de poche… petit par la taille mais grand par le talent » (quoted in a Mapstr comment).
- **July 2023** — Rue Hoche had become too small, so production and most of the team move to the new Hôtel-Dieu block at 2 passage Jeanne-Chauveau. The house adds a **bread range**. The baker is named in press as François Bonno; other press-named staff are a cheffe pâtissière and a chef pâtissier. Rue Hoche stays the pâtisserie and salon de thé.
- **c. 2025** — Ouest-France "Bretons en cuisine" interview: « Notre idée est de faire du 100 % maison, tout de A à Z » ([link](https://www.ouest-france.fr/societe/gastronomie/notre-idee-est-de-faire-du-100-pourcent-maison-tout-de-a-a-z-marion-juhel-la-patisserie-engagee-25ec5ee2-f8d6-11ef-a6a4-567d76e1951c), paywalled; exact date not confirmed).
- **Name:** 16h30 is goûter time. Customers make the link themselves: « 16h30, c'est l'heure du goûter » and « Avec l'heure du sacro-saint goûter pour enseigne… » (Mapstr comments).

## 8. Design direction

**Concept: « L'heure du goûter » — a site that runs on a clock.**
The whole site is organised around one dial that turns through the day towards 16:30, the moment the house is named after. The time of day drives the light, the copy and the vitrine. There are no wheat-sheaf or rustic-bakery clichés: this is a modern, sharp, slightly cheeky pâtisserie that cares about waste.

**Palette** — late-afternoon light over Rennes slate, using ingredients the house really works with:

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | crème de brioche | `#F6EEE1` |
| Ink | encre sarrasin | `#22191A` |
| Accent 1 | soleil de 16h30, amber | `#E39A3B` |
| Accent 2 | citron de Menton | `#EBD35A` (decorative only, never for text) |
| Accent 3 | épicéa / spruce | `#2E4A3C` |
| Secondary | beurre noisette | `#A8643A` |
| Neutral | ardoise de Rennes | `#5B6670` |

- Background colour shifts by hour: cool slate in the morning → warm amber around 16h30 → deep sarrasin in the evening.
- Contrast: ink on paper and paper on spruce both pass 4.5:1. Amber and citron are accents only, never body text.
- These are **proposed colours, not brand colours**. Replace them if the owner supplies the real identity.

**Type**
- *Instrument Serif* (display; italic for times and creation names)
- *Hanken Grotesk* (UI and body)
- A monospace with tabular figures for every time and price (e.g. *Martian Mono*)
- The numerals "16h30" are drawn as a custom SVG wordmark-style lockup. It is not a fake logo: it is clearly labelled as typographic.

**Signature interactions (unique to 16h30)**

1. **Le cadran du goûter (hero).** A large clock dial, pinned with a scroll-scrubbed timeline (GSAP ScrollTrigger). Scrolling moves the hand from 7h through « première fournée », « deuxième fournée » (viennoiseries twice a day — a real fact) and « mise en vitrine », and lands on **16:30**. At 16:30 the vitrine "opens" with a clip-path reveal and the light turns amber. On later visits the dial shows the visitor's real local time and a live countdown: « Goûter dans 2 h 14 ».
2. **La consigne (zero-waste mechanic).** On the "Engagements" page you drag a glass jar (SVG) back onto the counter. It snaps in with a spring, a coin flips "+1 €", and a counter of jars returned this season ticks up (mock number). This turns the real 1 € deposit scheme into something you can touch.
3. **L'atlas des créations.** The creations are named after places, so they are shown as a horizontal-scroll atlas: each card is a "destination" (Menton, Medellin, Bréhat, Cambrai…) with mock coordinates and a postcard flip that shows the ingredients.

**Motion language**
- Smooth scroll with Lenis.
- Time-of-day colour shifts using `@property`-animated custom properties.
- SplitText line reveals on creation names.
- View Transitions API for page changes: the clock hand sweeps as the wipe.
- A small marquee of the day's breads (Ty Fauve · Marcel · Lodève · Brasseur…).
- A magnetic "Commander pour 16h30" button.
- Animate transform and opacity only. Everything heavy is lazy-loaded.

**Reduced-motion fallback**
- The dial is shown fixed at 16:30, with no scrubbing.
- Crossfades replace clip-path reveals.
- The atlas becomes a normal grid.
- The jar can be "returned" with a simple button (no drag). The coin appears with no flip.
- Smooth scroll and View Transitions are off.
